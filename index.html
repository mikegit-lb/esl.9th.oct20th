Tips: speak for a full minute

Don't memorise

Examiners can spot a memorised script immediately. Learn phrases, not sentences — then adapt them to the moment.

Answer first (“Yes, I do”), then give one reason, then one example.

Half-minute shape

· → Answer (3–5 words)
· → Because… (your reason)
· → For example… (a true story!)

That tiny structure fills 45 seconds without sounding rehearsed.

Buy thinking time

“Let me think…”, “Well, it depends…”, “I've never thought about it, but…”

Silence hurts; thinking out loud doesn't. It's normal and natural speech.

Mistakes are fine

Keep going. A fluent answer with one small mistake scores higher than a perfect, two‑word answer.

Slow down, don't stop.
